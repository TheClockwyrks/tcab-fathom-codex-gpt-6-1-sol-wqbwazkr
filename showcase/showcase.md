# Fathom

You are a small light in a very deep dark. Swim the flooded corridors of Fathom, grazing tiny plankton lights and piecing together a maze that only appears when your glow touches it. Clear the last mote and dive deeper.

Every meal makes your light stronger. Your circle grows, remembered passages return from the dark, and the next junction becomes easier to read. But a brighter forager is easier to hunt. Sometimes the safest move is to stop eating and let your glow fade.

Send a sonar pulse around the rock to find a way forward. Watch its cyan crest split through the corridors and catch a hidden hunter. Listen carefully: something down here sends a pulse back. Ink can break a light hunter's pursuit, but it will not silence you.

An amber jellyfish might be a bonus, or the Lanternjaw wearing its disguise. The eyeless Gloamfin follows sound and loses speed at corners. The Flarefish gives you half a second of warning before it burns the darkness away. Learn their tells, keep a route in mind, and see how far below the surface you can survive.
